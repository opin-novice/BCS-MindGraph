# 09. Temporally Contrastive Distractor Generation

## Goal
Generate distractors that are plausible but clearly incorrect at the target time.

## Why This Is Important
A strong MCQ does not merely have a correct answer; it also contains distractors that are semantically credible but temporally wrong or contextually misleading.

## Contrastive Strategy
Use knowledge that was plausible at nearby times, or that belongs to neighboring entities, but is invalid under the target time condition.

## Implementation Principle
Distractors should be constructed relative to the specific historical fact state, not generated from generic misconceptions alone.

## Evaluation Measure
Distractor quality should be judged by plausibility, temporal mismatch, and overall exam realism.
