# 05. Topic Ontology and Topic-Grounded Knowledge Demand

## Goal
Build a structured topic ontology that drives both knowledge acquisition and generation.

## Why It Matters
A temporally grounded system must know not only what facts matter, but which topic areas are currently relevant to BCS GK.

## Ontology Components
- high-level domains
- topic subcategories
- fact or entity classes
- temporal relevance signals
- exam-prior probability indicators

## Knowledge Demand Logic
The system should identify which topics are likely to produce exam-relevant queries and then acquire or maintain the relevant facts for those domains.

## Implementation Principle
Topic grounding should not be a superficial label; it should guide fact selection, retrieval, and MCQ generation.

## Expected Benefit
This helps ensure that generated questions are not only factually correct but also aligned with the distribution of BCS-style exam interest.
