# 12. Paper Writing Plan

## Goal
Turn the implementation and experimentation into a publication-ready research paper.

## Likely Paper Structure
1. Introduction
2. Motivation and problem statement
3. Related work
4. Method
5. Data and temporal holdout policy
6. Knowledge graph and provenance design
7. Generation and verification architecture
8. Experiments and evaluation
9. Results
10. Discussion
11. Limitations and future work
12. Conclusion

## Core Storyline
The paper should tell a coherent narrative:
- BCS questions are time-sensitive
- static memory is insufficient
- temporal, provenance-rich memory improves generation quality
- verification is necessary for reliable exam-style generation

## Expected Contribution Statement
This paper contributes a time-aware, provenance-grounded MCQ generation system for BCS General Knowledge that demonstrates better factuality and temporal alignment under strict holdout evaluation.

## Final Paper Principle
The implementation, experiments, and writing should all support one central research claim: structured temporal knowledge maintenance materially improves fact-grounded and exam-relevant MCQ generation.
